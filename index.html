Profile
Medicine · Journalism · Science

I am a physician specialising in preventive medicine and nutritiology, a certified food technologist, and a food safety auditor (ISO 22000 / FSSC 22000). Since 2021 I have worked at the intersection of medicine and media — writing for national publications, producing expert commentary, and developing content strategies for health professionals and brands.

All my content is grounded in evidence-based medicine — I source from PubMed, Cochrane, The Lancet, and peer-reviewed clinical guidelines. I write under my own name and as a ghostwriter for other specialists: psychologists, sports medicine physicians, and nutritionists. I currently serve as a physician-methodologist and Head of Communications at the Centre for Public Health and Medical Prevention, Krasnodar Region, alongside independent copywriting and journalism practice.

"I bring clinical accuracy and journalistic readability to every piece — because health content that isn't read helps no one."
